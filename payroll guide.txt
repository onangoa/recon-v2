Phase 1: Configuration (One-time setup)

   1. Define Job Roles (Designations):
       * Go to Workers > Designations.
       * Click Add Designation (e.g., "Site Manager", "Mason").
       * Set the Min/Max Salary. The system uses the Min Salary as the base pay for payroll calculations.
   2. Set Up Pay Rules (Salary Components):
       * Go to Payroll > Components.
       * Add your standard Earnings (e.g., House Allowance) and Deductions (e.g., NHIF, NSSF).
       * Note: For statutory deductions like NHIF/NSSF, ensure you mark them as Pre-Tax so they are deducted before PAYE calculation.

  Phase 2: Workforce Management

   3. Register Your Workers:
       * Go to Workers > Add Worker.
       * Fill in their personal details, National ID, and phone number (crucial for M-Pesa payments).
       * Assign them a Designation from the list you created in Phase 1.

  Phase 3: The Payroll Cycle

   4. Create a Payroll Period:
       * Go to Payroll > New Period.
       * Define the name (e.g., "June 2026") and the date range (e.g., 1st to 30th).
       * Save to create a Draft period.
   5. Process Payroll:
       * Open your newly created period from the Payroll dashboard.
       * Click Process Payroll.
       * What happens now: The system automatically identifies all active workers, applies your salary components, calculates the progressive Kenyan
         PAYE tax, subtracts relief, and generates a Salary Slip for every worker.
   6. Review and Pay:
  Key Logic Tips:
   * PAYE Calculation: The system uses the latest Kenyan tax bands (10% to 35%) and applies the KES 2,400 monthly personal relief automatically.
   * Net Pay: Net Pay = (Basic + Allowances) - (Pre-tax Deductions + PAYE + Post-tax Deductions).

In this system, you do not manually add workers one by one to a payroll period. Instead, the system uses an Automatic Inclusion logic.

  Here is how it works:

  1. The "Active" Rule
  When you click "Process Payroll" on a period (e.g., "June 2026"), the system automatically scans your entire workforce. It will include every worker
  who meets these two criteria:
   * Status: Must be set to Active.
   * Designation: Must have a job role assigned (so the system knows their base salary).

  2. How to ensure a worker is included:
  If a worker is missing from your payroll list, follow these steps:
   1. Go to the Workers module.
   2. Find the worker in the list.
   3. Check their Status. If it's "Inactive" or "Terminated," they will be skipped.
   4. Ensure they have a Designation (e.g., "Mason").
   5. If you just added a new worker, simply go back to the Payroll Period and click Process Payroll again. The system will "Upsert" the data—meaning
      it will add the new worker and update existing ones without creating duplicates.

  3. Summary of the Workflow:
   * Step 1: Register your workers in the Workers module and set them to Active.
   * Step 2: Create a Payroll Period (it starts as a Draft).
   * Step 3: Click Process Payroll. 
   * Result: The system pulls all your Active workers, calculates their pay, and creates their Salary Slips automatically.

  Pro Tip: If a worker leaves mid-month, change their status to Inactive before you process the payroll to ensure they are excluded from the payment
  run.
